# Credits

Open Corneroids is a fan remake. It would not exist without the original game and the people who kept it alive.

## Corneroids

[Corneroids](http://www.corneroids.com/) was created by **Mikko Pulkki** (2012). Its world, its rules and its look are
his work.

## Mods and texture packs bundled with this project

| Pack | In `byoa/` | Authors |
|---|---|---|
| Corneroids Alpha 1.2.0 game data (itemset, textures, models, shaders) | `Corneroids-Alpha-1.2.0/Corneroids/Content` | Mikko Pulkki and the Corneroids team |
| CADNR v21, the "Chef Anon's DarthNox Rebalance" itemset | `v21/Corneroids/Content` | **DarthNox** (original DarthNox's Itemset), **Chef Anon** (rebalance), with thanks to Kippeth, Moosecraft and BDLS |
| "Random Stuff" itemset | `navItemset` | **Navar0nius** (Nav) |
| Sord's updated textures | `SordsTextures` | **Sord213** (vanilla textures by Mikko Pulkki) |

## Preservation notice

The game data and mods above are **not** part of this project's code and are **not** covered by its licence. They
belong to their authors. They are included, unmodified and without the original game's program, so that the game can
be played out of the box and these works are not lost. If you are one of the authors and want your work removed, open
an issue or contact the maintainer, and it will be removed promptly.
