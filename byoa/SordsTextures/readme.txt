How to install:
1. Navigate to your Corneroids\Content\Textures\Blocks folder
RECCOMENDED: Create a backup of your blockTextures.png and lightTextureAtlas.png files
2. Drag and drop the updated blockTextures.png and lightTextureAtlas.png into the folder
3. Accept any prompts
4. You're done!
